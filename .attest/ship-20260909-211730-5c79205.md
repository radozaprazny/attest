## minor — the maintainer's own address survives one commit behind its redaction

- **Evidence** — `50e9893:.attest/ship-20260901-143933-9621526.md` (blob, history only). At
  `HEAD` that line reads `<redacted 2026-09-04 — attest ADR-0040>`; the pre-redaction blob is
  still reachable, which is what `full` mode exists to see and what a redaction in an
  append-only log can never fix on its own.
- **Weighing** — it is the **maintainer's own** address, and the same value is in the author
  field of 45 of the 64 commits, so publishing exposes nothing the commit metadata does not
  already carry in the open. No third party, no Art 9 class. Minor per the ladder's own rule
  that a maintainer's own identity in a repo they are publishing is noted, not alarmed about.
- **Remediation: none, and the reason is recorded rather than assumed.** Purging it would mean
  `git filter-repo` and a force-push, which rewrites every sha — and the shas are load-bearing
  here: `.attest/` record filenames name them, `ship_guard.sh` resolves them, and
  `template-cleanup.sh` discriminates an adopter's records from attest's by whether the sha in
  the name resolves (ADR-0041). The trade is a rewrite of the whole audit trail against an
  address that stays public in authorship regardless. ADR-0040's visible mark at `HEAD` remains
  the right treatment; what this record adds is that the mark is a statement, not an erasure.
- note: written in its own step, before the push it gates (ADR-0033), so it is untracked as the
  guard reads it and will land under a later sha than the one it names.
